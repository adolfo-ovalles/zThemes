## Working with PSDs

### Accessing Icon Names

All icon filenames are built into the PSD layers and layer comps. To ease navigation:

1. Open the **Layer Comps Panel** in Photoshop. Go to top menu bar
    <pre>Window → Layer Comps</pre>

2. Browse through the layer comps to see each icon and its corresponding filename

### Exporting Icons

<pre>Method ➡️ File → Export → Layer Comps to Files...</pre>

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
> | Setting | Value |
> | --- | --- |
> | `Format` | `🟢 PNG-24` |
> | `Transparency` | `🟢 Enabled` |
> | `Prefix` | `🔴 None` |
> | `ICC Profile` | `🔴 Disabled` |
> | `Interlaced` | `🔴 Disabled` |
